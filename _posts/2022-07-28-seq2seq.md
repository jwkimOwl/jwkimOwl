## 1. Structure of Sequence-to-Sequence
Unlike simple artificial neural networks, RNN is not a Feed Forward Neural Network. Feed Forward Neural Network only sends its output value forward, to the output layer. RNN, on the other hand, also sends its result of the activation function to its hidden layer's next calculation.
