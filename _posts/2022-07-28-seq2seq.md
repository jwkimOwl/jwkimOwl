# Sequence-to-Sequence (seq2seq)
Sequence-to-Sequence(seq2seq) is sequence model with an encoder and a decoder. It is used in various fields to obtain sequences from input sequences, such as chatbots and machine traslations.

Below is the contents of this post.
1. Structure of Sequence-to-Sequence
2. reference

## 1. Structure of Sequence-to-Sequence
Let's look at how Sequence-to-Sequence models look like.

![Seq2Seq_img1.png](/jwkimOwl/assets/img/Seq2Seq_img1.png)

As you can see above, the encoder receives a sequence (words in a English sentence) to compress them into a one vector. This vector is called a context vector. Then, the decoder receives the context vector to return the final sequence (French words). Encoder can be made with an RNN cell or, by performance issues, usually LSTM or GRU cells.
